The Riverstone Experience

Elegant spaces,
warm conversations,
meals worth lingering over.

Nine floors above Kharadi, Riverstone is a fine-dine restaurant, a boho-chic bar and a club in one address — carved stone, sculpted walls and warm light, open from noon until half past one in the morning.

The kitchen runs across North Indian, Continental, Oriental and European menus. The bar pours its own Signature Rituals. And on the right night, the lights drop and the DJ takes over.
